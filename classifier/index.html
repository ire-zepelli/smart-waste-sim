    How this works

    1. Tap a bin. Its camera opens.
    2. Hold an item to the camera and press Present item.
    3. The classifier decides, then publishes over MQTT.
    4. The ESP32 unlocks that lid only on a match — otherwise it stays locked and the red LED lights.

    Capacity comes from the ultrasonic sensors. A bin at 85% reports FULL and refuses everything, regardless of what you present.
